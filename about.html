About Our Holding

AKYLGROUP is a leading development company in Kazakhstan, delivering high-quality residential and commercial real estate. Our primary goal is to create sustainable infrastructure and comfortable living conditions for everyone. We constantly implement innovative construction technologies and modern eco-friendly materials.

Company Timeline

  1. 2014 — Founding of AKYLGROUP construction holding.
  2. 2017 — Completion of the first major residential complex.
  3. 2021 — Expansion into commercial property development.
  4. 2024 — Launch of green building standards across all projects.
  5. 2026 — Reaching over 15,000 satisfied homeowner families.

Key Performance Facts

  • Over 45 completed high-rise residential complexes.
  • More than 1,000,000 square meters of urban space built.
  • 98% of projects completed strictly on schedule.
  • 12 industry awards for architectural innovation.
